# Swiggy Food Delivery Sales & Restaurant Performance Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Swiggy Food Delivery Sales & Restaurant Performance Dashboard** built using **Microsoft Excel and Power Pivot**.

The dashboard analyzes **197K+ food orders** to provide insights into sales performance, customer ratings, order trends, food types, geographic distribution, and restaurant performance.

The primary objective was to transform raw food delivery data into an interactive business dashboard that can be used to identify trends, compare performance, and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The objective of this project is to analyze Swiggy's food delivery data and answer key business questions such as:

- How are sales performing over time?
- Which days and months generate the highest sales?
- How does sales performance vary across food types?
- Which states and cities contribute the most revenue?
- How do sales, ratings, and orders vary by quarter?
- Which cities are the top contributors to overall sales?
- How do sales fluctuate throughout the week?
- What is the overall customer rating and average order value?

---

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard tracks the following key metrics:

- **Total Sales:** ₹53.01M
- **Average Rating:** 4.34
- **Average Order Value:** ₹268.51
- **Rating Count:** 5.59M
- **Total Orders:** 197.43K

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### Monthly Sales Trend
Shows how total sales fluctuate from month to month and helps identify periods of higher and lower sales performance.

### Daily Sales Trend
Highlights variations in sales across different days of the week.

### Sales by Food Type
Compares revenue contribution between **Vegetarian and Non-Vegetarian** food orders.

### Sales by State
Uses a map visualization to display the geographic distribution of sales across Indian states.

### Quarterly Performance Summary
Compares **Sales, Average Rating, and Total Orders** across quarters.

### Top 5 Cities by Sales
Identifies the cities contributing the highest amount of revenue.

### Weekly Sales Trend
Analyzes sales fluctuations across individual weeks to identify consistency and peak periods.
